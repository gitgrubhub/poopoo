

Applicant Responsibilities

Please describe this applicant's current leadership responsibilities as they pertain to your department/division.

Dr. Pham leads two teams critical to the MGH Department of Psychiatry. First, as a NCCIH K23 awardee and NIA Resource Center for Minority Aging Research Scholar, he leads a multidisciplinary research team that culturally adapts known behavioral interventions for the chronic pain needs of older Black Americans in the Greater Boston area. This team includes prominent community partners, senior investigators, fellow researchers, clinical research coordinators, and administrators.  Second, as an MGPO Building Communities and Connection Grant recipient, he leads a multidisciplinary team that promotes connection and community among MGB Asian American and Pacific Islander physicians. This team includes a program manager along with other MGB physicians. Both of these team projects focus on health disparities, culture, and community, subjects that absolutely align with our department's mission to improve diversity, equity, and inclusion.
